1. I won't add Google Analytics tracking, I don't want to support the advertising/monetization empire. 
2. I also won't add any other analytics tracking or ad links. I don't need to know who visits my site, and the enjoyment of looking at numbers is not worth the risk of me storing your data. 
3. I won't add comments. If you want to talk about it you can send me a message some other way. 
4. If I get doxed again the site comes down again. This is only here as long as I'm glad it's here. 
5. Anything I want to blog about is fair game. In other words this is not and never will be a work account, though I may collect things I have written about or for work.
